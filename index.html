05 — Process

A guided path from first message to launch.

  1. Request a consultation

    Tell us about the business, your current web presence, and what customers should do.

  2. Fit and direction review

    VIM Webworks reviews the public business presence, confirms package fit, and identifies missing information.

  3. Written scope and deposit

    Package, price, exclusions, revision rounds, responsibilities, and timing are confirmed in writing. Production begins only after the approved 50% reservation deposit and complete intake.

  4. Reference and content lock

    Approved business facts, assets, examples, and visual direction are consolidated before the build.

  5. Private build and refinement

    The responsive draft is built, technically checked, and shared privately. Feedback is provided as one consolidated list per included round.

  6. Approval, final payment, and launch

    Public launch or transfer occurs only after factual approval and the remaining payment conditions are satisfied.

  7. Optional Website Care

    Maintenance is offered under separately accepted monthly terms.
